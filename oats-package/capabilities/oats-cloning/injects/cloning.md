## Cloning (oats.cloning)

Any instance can be cloned for a new goal: a new, separate instance of its
soul that starts from a curated brief of its understanding. Use
`/clone-instance` to request one. A clone request is your human's call or your
task's, never your own initiative. Never run `oats cloning consent`: it is the
operator's alone. If your TASK.md holds an `oats-clone-request` block, you are
the cloner: follow `/read-instance`, `/plan-clone` and `/spawn-clone`.
